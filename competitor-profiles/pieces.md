## Positioning

Pieces promises a broad, always-on memory layer spanning apps, websites, meetings, and AI tools. Its scope extends well beyond developer sessions into general knowledge work, and it offers search, timelines, generated recaps, and MCP access to history.

## Strengths

- Broader recall surface and polished “memory layer” category story.
- On-device storage, source controls, and selective deletion address privacy concerns.
- Clear individual-to-enterprise expansion path.
- Can generate morning briefs, day recaps, and other reusable artifacts.

## Weaknesses relative to CapsStack

- Always-on capture is broader and may feel more invasive than an explicitly bounded away interval.
- The product is not specialized around terminal-agent return, blockers, and next-review actions.
- Paid plan starts before long-term value is proven; the trial requires a card.

## Competitive implication for CapsStack

Do not compete on “remember everything.” Own the narrower, explainable job: **a deliberate return brief for agent work completed while you were away**. CapsStack can be lighter, more inspectable, and less capture-heavy, but Pieces is much stronger if the user wants general recall across their entire digital day.
