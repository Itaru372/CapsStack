## Positioning

Vibe Kanban treats planning and review as the new engineering bottleneck. It provides a board, isolated workspaces/worktrees, agent execution, diff review, comments, preview, and pull-request workflows.

## Strengths

- Complete plan-to-review interface rather than a single summary artifact.
- Strong visual task status, diff review, and parallel-worktree workflow.
- Broad agent support and substantial public adoption signals.
- Messaging validates the same human bottleneck identified in CapsStack customer research.

## Weaknesses relative to CapsStack

- Heavier workflow migration: users must plan and run work inside its issue/workspace model.
- Larger implementation and trust surface than an interval-scoped return utility.
- Commercial operation failed despite usage; paid team value was not strong enough for the company.
- Community maintenance creates roadmap and support uncertainty.

## Competitive implication for CapsStack

Vibe Kanban validates planning/review pain but warns against equating usage with willingness to pay. CapsStack should stay focused and integrate with existing agent habits rather than becoming another orchestration dashboard. A sustainable business model needs validation before building team administration.
