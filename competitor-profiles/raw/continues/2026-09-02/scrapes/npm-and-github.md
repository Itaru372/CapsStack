## Product claims captured

- Positioning: resume an AI coding session in another tool after interruption or rate limiting.
- `npx continues` discovers local sessions, parses native formats, extracts conversation/file/tool state, creates a structured handoff, and launches or prompts the target tool.
- The npm page lists 16 supported coding agents and 240 source-to-target paths.
- Offers same-tool native resume, cross-tool handoff, inspection, listing, JSON/JSONL, and bulk dump commands.
- Node.js 22.5+ is required according to current documentation.
- MIT licensed and free/open source.
- GitHub showed approximately 1.4k stars during the 2026-09-02 scan.

## Limits

- No paid pricing, customer case studies, independent review score, or SEO metrics were found.
- The product's core job is agent-to-agent continuity, not a human return brief after an away interval.
