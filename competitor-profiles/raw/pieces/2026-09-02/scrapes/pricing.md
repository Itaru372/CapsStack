# Pieces pricing — raw research notes

**Retrieved:** 2026-09-02  
**Source:** https://pieces.app/pricing

## Pricing captured

- Pro: $18.99 per user/month, billed monthly; annual option advertised at 25% savings.
- Enterprise: $22.99 per user/month, billed monthly; $69.99 per user/quarter also listed.
- New users: 7-day trial, card required; 14-day money-back guarantee.
- No free plan is offered.
- Pro includes on-device long-term memory, cross-app capture, summaries/recaps, model access, and MCP history access.
- Enterprise adds organization-wide controls, approved providers, and bring-your-own-key support.
