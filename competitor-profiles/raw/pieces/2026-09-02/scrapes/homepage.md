# Pieces homepage — raw research notes

**Retrieved:** 2026-09-02  
**Source:** https://pieces.app/

## Page claims captured

- Headline: “The memory layer for everything you do.”
- Runs in the background and forms a searchable memory of prior activity.
- Captures activity across email, browser, chat, design, coding, and meeting tools.
- Offers timeline, scoped search, one-click summaries/recaps, and MCP delivery into AI assistants.
- Says memories are saved on-device by default and capture can be paused or filtered by app/source.
- Primary CTA: start a 7-day free trial; card required.

## Limits

- Visual quality and conversion performance were not independently measured.
- Customer counts, traffic, backlinks, and review data were not available from the page.
