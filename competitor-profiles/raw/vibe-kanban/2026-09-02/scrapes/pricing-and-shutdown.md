# Vibe Kanban pricing and shutdown — raw research notes

**Retrieved:** 2026-09-02  
**Sources:**

- https://www.vibekanban.com/pricing
- https://www.vibekanban.com/blog/shutdown
- https://github.com/BloopAI/vibe-kanban

## Pricing page captured

- Free: $0/month for one individual user.
- Pro: $30/user/month for teams of 2–49, previously including SLA and Discord support.
- Enterprise: custom price for 50+ users, previously including SSO/SAML and higher SLA.

## Shutdown announcement captured

- Bloop, the company behind Vibe Kanban, shut down on 2026-04-10.
- The project continues as open source and community maintained.
- The announcement says thousands used it daily, but the vast majority were free users and the company did not find a compelling business model.
- Subscriptions were terminated and remote services scheduled for removal; local workspaces remain.
- GitHub showed roughly 28k stars during the 2026-09-02 scan.
